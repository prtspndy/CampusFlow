# CampusFlow Design System

The design language for the **Student Organization System**: one platform that runs a campus club's members, events, merchandise, volunteers and money. It is written for AI design and coding agents as much as for people. Every value is a token above, and every rule says when to use it and, just as important, when not to.

## Overview

### What we are designing for

The Skyline Student Association is run by students with classes to attend. Nobody on the leadership team is a trained admin, and everybody is on a phone. The old way of working (a spreadsheet, a cash box, a notebook, WhatsApp groups) fails in one particular way: **nobody can see the whole picture at a glance.** CampusFlow exists to fix that, so the visual design has one job: make the state of the club obvious in under five seconds.

### Personality

Friendly, capable, a little bit proud. It should feel like a well-run student union, not a bank and not a toy. Think *confident clarity with a warm accent*.

### Brand and aesthetic

CampusFlow bridges **operational rigour with collegiate vibrance**: the institutional trust that accounting and ticketing demand, with the warmth people expect from campus life. The aesthetic is *Pragmatic Utility and Layered Physical Metaphors*.

- **Clarity over decoration.** High information density without clutter, in the spirit of well-made productivity tools and modern financial apps.
- **Physical tangibility.** Digital objects borrow cues from real ones: the membership pass has card proportions and a top accent stripe, the ticket has a notched stub and a dashed perforation, and every scannable code sits on its own high-contrast tile.
- **Strict visual restraint.** Skyline Blue is reserved for navigation and forward action, never for status badges or ambient fills. Tabular figures govern the accounting. Modules keep their colour identity.
- **Operational adaptability.** The admin console and member app respect the system colour mode. The door scanner is the exception: it stays in an uncompromising high-contrast dark state, readable at arm's length in a dim venue.

### Design principles

1. **Glanceable first.** Every screen answers one question ("Who's coming?", "What do we still owe?", "What's left to do?") in its top third.
2. **One primary action per screen.** One filled `{colors.primary}` button in view, at most. Everything else is secondary or ghost.
3. **Money is always legible and never frightening.** Tabular figures, explicit signs, no red for ordinary spending. Red is for an overdrawn balance or a failed payment.
4. **Each part of the club has a colour.** Members, events, announcements, shop, tasks and treasury each own a pastel tint. The tint appears on cards, icons and section headers so users learn where they are without reading.
5. **Status is never colour alone.** Every status pairs colour with a word and, where useful, an icon.
6. **Phone first, thumb sized.** Touch targets are at least 44px. The member, ticket and check-in flows are designed at 390px wide before anything else.
7. **Tickets look like tickets.** Real-world metaphors (perforated stub, membership pass) beat abstract cards where the object has a physical equivalent.

## Themes: Light and Dark

CampusFlow ships two complete themes with identical token names. `colors` is the light theme and `colors-dark` is the dark theme; `elevation` and `elevation-dark` work the same way. **Components only ever reference semantic tokens** (`{colors.surface}`, `{colors.ink}`, `{colors.primary}`), so a theme is applied by swapping the token map, never by editing components.

### Behaviour

- The default follows the device (`prefers-color-scheme`). The profile menu offers **System, Light, Dark**, and the choice is remembered per person.
- **Door Check-in opens in dark** regardless of the setting, because it is used in dim halls. Everything else follows the person's choice.
- The Member Pass looks the same in both themes: it is a navy card with a hairline edge in dark so it still separates from the page.
- Switching themes is an instant swap with no animation, so nothing flashes.

### Rules for dark

1. **Depth comes from lighter surfaces and borders, not shadows.** Step up `canvas` → `surface` → `surface-raised`, add a `hairline` edge, and keep the shadow tokens only as a faint grounding.
2. **Never pure black or pure white.** The page is `#0b1020`, and the main text is `#f2f4ff`. Pure values vibrate on OLED screens.
3. **Colour gets lighter and softer, not louder.** Brand, status and money colours are lifted so they hold contrast on dark; tints become deep washes with light text.
4. **Text on a filled primary button flips to navy** (`on-primary`). Do not hard-code white on `primary`.
5. **A filled ink chip flips too**: the active filter chip is light on dark, with `on-ink` text.
6. **Photos dim slightly** (about 8% black overlay) and never sit on a pure white tile, except QR codes, which always stay black on white for scanning.
7. **Charts and the category bar keep the same hue order** in both themes, using each module's text colour in dark and its fill colour in light.
8. **Illustrations** swap to their dark variant with the same shapes; do not tint a light illustration.

## Token Compatibility (Material and Stitch)

The front matter carries two layers that never contradict each other. The **CampusFlow semantic tokens** (`primary`, `ink`, `canvas`, `module tints`, `money-*`) are what components use. The **Material-style role tokens** (`surface-container-*`, `on-surface`, `outline`, `secondary`, `tertiary`, `*-fixed`) are aliases for tools that expect Material or Stitch naming. Both exist in the light and dark maps.

### Role mapping

| Material / Stitch role | Use it for | Closest CampusFlow token |
|---|---|---|
| `background`, `surface` | Page and app background | `canvas`, `surface` |
| `surface-container-lowest` → `-highest` | Stepped layers: cards, raised panels, inputs on tinted areas | `canvas` → `surface-raised` / `surface-sunken` |
| `on-surface`, `on-surface-variant` | Primary and secondary text | `ink` and `body` / `muted` |
| `outline`, `outline-variant` | Control borders, dividers | `hairline-strong`, `hairline` |
| `primary`, `on-primary` | Filled action and its label | `primary`, `on-primary` |
| `primary-container`, `on-primary-container` | Prominent branded surfaces such as a hero card | `primary` fill with its light label |
| `secondary*` | The Sunset family: ribbons, date highlights, credential stripe | `sunset`, `sunset-deep`, `sunset-tint` |
| `tertiary*` | The green family: paid, active, positive | `success`, `success-tint`, `success-deep` |
| `error*` | Failures and destructive actions | `error`, `error-tint`, `error-deep` |
| `inverse-surface`, `inverse-on-surface`, `inverse-primary` | Snackbars and toasts on the opposite theme | `toast` styling |
| `*-fixed`, `*-fixed-dim` | Theme-independent accents that must not change between light and dark | Used as is, identical in both themes |

### Decisions where the sources disagreed

1. **Primary colour.** The Stitch front matter listed `primary` as `#003ece` while its own prose and the earlier file both name `#2457f5` as the brand blue. **`primary` is `#2457f5`.** The darker `#003ece` is kept as `{colors.primary-strong}`, used for link hover and pressed text on light surfaces where extra contrast is wanted (`#c7d3ff` in dark).
2. **Radius scale.** The Stitch front matter used the Material defaults (4, 8, 12, 16, 24), but its prose and the earlier file define 4, 6, 10, 14, 20. **The prose scale wins**, and `rounded.DEFAULT` is an alias of `md` (10px).
3. **Ticket stub orientation.** Phone-first, so the ticket is stacked (event on top, QR below, horizontal perforation, side notches). From 640px up it may switch to the horizontal form (vertical perforation, top and bottom notches).
4. **Sunset values.** `sunset` is `#ff6b4a`. The Material alias `secondary-container` keeps the provided `#fd6a49`, a one-step difference that is visually identical. Prefer `sunset` in components.
5. **Tablet navigation.** A collapsible drawer, not an icon rail, so labels stay readable.

### Contrast

Every text role pair (`on-*` on its container) meets 4.5:1 in both themes. `outline` is for control borders and meets 3:1 (UI components). `outline-variant` and `hairline` are decorative dividers and are not relied on to identify a control.

### Money type

`money-xl`, `money-lg` and `money-md` are Inter with `font-feature-settings: "tnum" 1` so digits align in columns. **Every amount in the product uses a money token**, in tables, tiles, receipts, checkout and tickets. Right-align amounts in tables. Always show two decimals in ledgers, and format with `Intl.NumberFormat` using the club's configured currency and locale. Never hard-code a currency symbol.

### Principles

- Headlines are heavy (700-800) with slightly negative tracking; body is regular. Do not use weight 500 for headlines.
- Sentence case everywhere, apart from `micro-uppercase` overlines.
- Line length for reading text caps at about 68 characters.
- Do not set text below 13px, apart from the 11px uppercase overline.

## Layout

### Spacing

A 4px base. Use the named scale (`xxs` 4, `xs` 8, `sm` 12, `md` 16, `lg` 20, `xl` 24, `xxl` 32, `xxxl` 40). Cards pad at `xl`; dense list rows pad at `sm md`. Sections on the public site separate at `section` (64px), and hero bands at `section-lg` (96px).

### Margins and gutters

Page margin is `margin-mobile` (16px) on phones, `margin` (24px) on tablet and `margin-desktop` (40px) on desktop. Gutters between columns are `gutter-mobile` (16px) then `gutter` (24px). The admin sidebar is `sidebar-width` (248px); admin content caps at `content-max` (1280px), the public site at `public-max` (1200px), and the member app at `member-max` (640px).

### Grid and containers

| Context | Container | Columns |
|---|---|---|
| Public site | max 1200px, 24px gutters | 12 |
| Member app | max 640px centred on desktop, full width on phone | 1 |
| Admin console | sidebar 248px + fluid content, content max 1280px | 12 |
| Door check-in | full screen | 1 |

### Admin shell

Left sidebar (`nav-sidebar`) with the six modules and the active item on `{colors.primary-tint}`. A top bar holds the global search pill and a profile menu. Page content starts with a `heading-1` title, then **a row of `stat-tile`s** that answer the page's key question, then the working list or table.

### Whitespace

Be generous on public and member surfaces (the Airbnb lesson: let photography and space do the work). Be compact but never cramped in the admin console: 56px rows, 16px between cards.

### Rhythm on the public site

Alternate surface bands so the page paces itself: white, then a tinted module band, then white, then a navy band, then white. Never stack two identical bands.

## Elevation, Shape and Depth

- **Level 0**: flat, no border, for page backgrounds.
- **Level 1** (`{elevation.level-1}`): event cards and resting cards that need to feel tappable.
- **Level 2** (`{elevation.level-2}`): ticket stub, dropdowns, hovered cards.
- **Level 3** (`{elevation.level-3}`): the member pass, modals, toasts, and any element that floats over the page.
- **Focus ring** (`{elevation.focus-ring}`): 3px translucent blue, on every focusable element, always visible on keyboard focus.

Prefer borders and tinted surfaces to shadows for ordinary hierarchy. Shadows are for things that float or can be picked up.

**Layering by theme.** In light, crisp white cards sit on the cool `surface` page with a `hairline` border, plus soft navy-tinted ambient shadows (`rgba(15, 22, 48, …)`). In dark, depth is stepped illumination instead of shadow: `canvas` → `surface` → `surface-raised`, with a stronger `hairline` so containers never bleed into each other.

### Radius

`rounded.xs` (4px) for progress-track caps and tiny inner indicators; `rounded.sm` (6px) for date blocks and nested thumbnails; `rounded.md` (10px) for buttons, inputs, search fields, alerts and banners; `rounded.lg` (14px) for cards; `rounded.xl` (20px) for the ticket stub, member pass and modals; `rounded.full` for chips, status badges, search and avatars. **Buttons are never pills.** That keeps the pill shape meaning "a filter, a status or a search".

### Iconography and imagery

- Icons: 24px, 1.75px rounded stroke, outlined (a Lucide-style set). Filled variants mean *selected* only.
- Event cards lead with a photo (4:3). If there is none, use a navy to blue gradient with the event's date block, never an empty grey box.
- Illustration is light, flat, in the module tint palette, and is used for empty states and the hero only.
- Avatars are circular. Without a photo, show initials on the member's module tint.

### Motion

`fast` 120ms for hover and press, `base` 200ms for most transitions, `slow` 320ms for sheet and modal entrances, all on `{motion.easing}`. Success moments (ticket purchased, member checked in) get a single short scale-in on the confirmation, never confetti loops. Respect `prefers-reduced-motion` by replacing movement with a fade.

## Components

### Buttons

- **Primary** (`button-primary`): filled `{colors.primary}`, 44px tall, 10px radius. One per screen.
- **Secondary** (`button-secondary`): white with a `{colors.hairline-strong}` outline. The usual partner for primary.
- **Ghost** (`button-ghost`): text-only, `{colors.primary-deep}`, for tertiary actions such as "Cancel" or "View all". On hover it fills with `{colors.primary-tint}` (`button-ghost-hover`).
- **Danger** (`button-danger`): only for irreversible actions (delete event, refund order), and always behind a confirmation modal.
- **On dark** (`button-on-dark`): white fill, navy text, for hero and member pass areas.
- **States**: hover shifts to `{colors.primary-pressed}`; disabled uses `button-primary-disabled`; loading replaces the label with a spinner of the same width so the layout never jumps.
- Full-width on phones for the main action in a flow (Buy ticket, Join, Place order).

### Inputs and forms

- 48px tall, 16px text, 10px radius, `{colors.hairline-strong}` border. Focus swaps to a 2px primary border with the focus ring. Errors use a 2px `{colors.error}` border plus a message below with an icon.
- The **search pill** (`search-pill`) is 48px tall with 20px side padding and an 18px leading search icon, on `{colors.surface}` with a `hairline` border.
- Labels sit **above** fields in `body-sm-medium`; never use a placeholder as the label.
- Group related fields in a `card-base`; one column on phones, a maximum of two on desktop.
- Pick the right control: segmented chips for 2-5 choices, select for more, date picker for dates, size picker for apparel (see Shop).
- Validate on blur, not on every keystroke. Keep the user's input on error.

### Cards

- `card-base` for generic content. `card-module-*` for module hubs and dashboards. `card-event` and `card-product` for photo-led browsing, with a 14px clipped image on top, a 16px content block below, and the price right-aligned.
- Module cards tint their header, icon and key figure in the matching `-deep` colour, so the module is recognisable from across the screen.
- Entire card is the tap target. Do not nest a second primary button inside a browsing card.

### Navigation

- **Phone**: a five-item `nav-bottom-tab` (Home, Events, Pass, Shop, More). The Pass tab is always one tap away.
- **Desktop admin**: a 248px `nav-sidebar` with module icons in their tint colours. The active item uses `nav-sidebar-item-active`: a `{colors.primary-tint}` fill, `{colors.primary-deep}` text and a 3px vertical pill indicator on its left edge.
- **Tablet**: the sidebar becomes a collapsible drawer opened from a menu button.
- **Tabs** (`tab`, `tab-active`) for switching views within a page (for example Upcoming, Past, Drafts). **Filter chips** (`chip-filter`, `chip-filter-active`) for narrowing a list.
- Breadcrumbs only when the page is at least three levels deep.

### Status badges

**Filter chips** are 36px tall visually but keep a 44px touch target through an invisible hit area. **Status badges** are always a pill, always a word. `badge-active` (Active, Paid, Checked in), `badge-expiring` (Expires in 12 days, Low stock), `badge-expired` (Expired, Failed), `badge-neutral` (Draft, Pending), `badge-member-price` (Member price). Add a 12px leading icon when the list is dense.

### Tables and lists

- A **ledger row** has two clear columns: the transaction name in `body-sm` over its date in `caption` on the left, and the amount on the right in `money-md`, `{colors.money-in}` for deposits and `{colors.money-out}` for ordinary outflows.
- Header row on `{colors.surface-sunken}`, 13px `micro-uppercase` labels. Rows are 56px with a hairline divider (`ledger-row`, `task-row`).
- Sticky header, sortable columns shown with a caret. Money columns right-aligned in `money-md`.
- On phones, tables become stacked list cards. Never use horizontal scroll for key data.
- Bulk actions appear in a bar that replaces the header when rows are selected.

### Feedback

- **Toast** (`toast`): bottom-centre on phones, bottom-left on desktop, 4 seconds, with an Undo action where the change is reversible.
- **Banners** (`banner-info`, `banner-warning`): inline at the top of the relevant page, for things like "Your membership expires in 12 days" or "Gala has 8 seats left".
- **Modal** (`modal`): for confirmation and focused tasks. On phones it becomes a bottom sheet.
- **Empty states**: an illustration, one sentence saying what belongs here, and one primary action (see Content).

## Signature Components

### Member Pass

A digital membership card, and the heart of the member app.

- A `member-pass` on `{colors.brand-navy}`, 20px radius and Level 3 shadow, with a 6px `{colors.sunset}` bar along the top edge.
- Top-left: the club mark. Top-right: a status badge (**Active**, **Expiring** or **Expired**).
- Centre: the member's name in `heading-2` on-dark, the member ID in `code`, and a large QR code on a white tile with 12px padding and 12px radius.
- Footer: `Valid until 31 Dec` and the member's perks as small chips ("Member ticket prices", "Merch discount").
- **Expiring** (30 days or fewer) adds a `banner-warning` above the pass with a primary **Renew** button. **Expired** greys the QR with a "Renew to use your pass" overlay.
- The pass must work offline once loaded, and brightens the screen when opened.

### Event card and ticket stub

- **Event card** (`card-event`): a 4:3 photo with a white `date-block` overlay (6px radius, month in `micro-uppercase`, day in `heading-2`) at top-left; below it the title, venue and time, then the price row. The price row shows the **member price** next to a `badge-member-price`, and the standard price in `money-md` with a muted strike-through for members who are signed in.
- A `seat-meter` (track `{colors.surface-sunken}`, fill `{colors.primary}`) shows seats left. It turns `{colors.warning}` when 10% or fewer remain, and the label changes to "Only 8 left". Sold out replaces the buy button with a "Join the waitlist" secondary button.
- **Ticket stub** (`ticket-stub`, 20px radius, Level 2): stacked on phones. The top section carries event name, date, venue, ticket type and holder. A horizontal dashed `{colors.hairline-strong}` perforation runs across the middle, with a 12px semicircular notch cut inward from the left and right edges. The bottom section holds the QR tile and the `ticket-code`. From 640px up it may switch to a horizontal ticket with a vertical perforation and top and bottom notches. The stub is what the buyer sees after checkout and in their wallet. Offer "Add to Apple Wallet" and "Add to Google Wallet" as secondary buttons.

### Door Check-in

Built for a volunteer holding a phone in a dim hall.

- The screen opens straight to the camera with a manual code entry field beneath it. No menus.
- A scan fills the whole screen with one of three results, each with a large icon, a `checkin-result` headline and the holder's name and ticket type:
  - **Valid** on `{colors.checkin-valid}`: "Welcome in, Aanya", and a haptic tick.
  - **Already used** on `{colors.checkin-used}`: "Scanned at 7:42 pm", with a **Let in anyway** secondary button that logs an override.
  - **Invalid** on `{colors.checkin-invalid}`: "Ticket not found", with a manual lookup action.
- The result stays for 1.5 seconds, then the camera resumes automatically. A running counter ("142 of 180 in") stays pinned at the top.
- High contrast, 56px minimum buttons, no animation beyond the result fade.

### Money summary and ledger

- A row of three `stat-tile`s: **Money in**, **Money out**, **Balance**. Values in `money-lg`, with a `micro-uppercase` label and a small delta ("+120.00 this week"). Balance is `ink` when positive and `{colors.money-negative}` only when below zero.
- Under it, a ledger of `ledger-row`s: date, description, category chip (Dues, Tickets, Merch, Fundraiser, Expense), linked source (event, order or receipt), and an amount in `money-md` with `+` or `−`.
- A category breakdown uses a horizontal stacked bar (not a pie), in module tints, with a legend that repeats the category names. The totals for dues, tickets, merchandise and reimbursements are always visible at the top, because the treasurer's job is to explain *where the money went*.
- **Reimbursements**: a volunteer uploads a receipt photo and amount; the row moves through `Submitted`, `Approved`, `Paid` using `badge-neutral`, `badge-expiring` and `badge-active`. Receipts open in a modal beside the ledger entry.
- Offer "Export for the semester" (CSV and PDF) as a secondary button.

### Announcement composer and feed

- The composer is a single card: title, rich-text body, audience chips (All members, Volunteers, Event attendees), channel toggles (Website, Email), and a primary **Send** button that shows a recipient count ("Send to 214 members").
- The feed is a vertical list of cards on `{colors.tint-lavender}` headers with the date, author and a "Sent to 214 · Opened by 171" line. Announcements stay searchable, so nothing gets lost in a chat again.

### Shop and size picker

- A `card-product` grid, 2-up on phones, 4-up on desktop. Price in `money-md`; members see their discounted price with a `badge-member-price`.
- The **size picker** is a row of chips (XS to XXL). A size with no stock is disabled with a diagonal strike and a "Notify me" link. A size with 5 or fewer left shows `badge-expiring` ("3 left").
- The cart is a bottom sheet on phones and a right drawer on desktop, and checkout is one page with a single primary **Pay** button that shows the total.
- In admin, a stock table lists each size with an inline editable quantity and a low-stock threshold.

### Fundraiser and task board

- A fundraiser page opens with a progress card (`card-module-tasks`): task completion as a bar, days to go, a goal amount against money raised in `money-lg`.
- Tasks appear as `task-row`s grouped by owner or by status (To do, In progress, Done), with an avatar, due date chip, and a circular checkbox that fills `{colors.success}` when done. Overdue due dates turn `{colors.error-deep}` with an icon and the word "Overdue".
- A **board view** offers three columns for those who prefer cards. The default on phones is the list.
- An "Unassigned" group always sits at the top so gaps are visible, such as nobody yet owning the supplies.

### Renewal reminder

A `banner-warning` at the top of the member app 30 days before expiry, then at 7 days. It is dismissible until the next threshold, carries one **Renew** button and states the date in full. The same reminder is sent by email with the same wording.

## Content and Voice

- **Tone**: a helpful senior student. Plain, friendly, specific. No jargon, no corporate filler, no exclamation overload (one per screen, at most).
- **Say what happened and what's next.** "You're in! Your pass is ready." beats "Success."
- **Errors are kind and fixable.** "That ticket isn't in our list. Check the code or look up the name." Never blame the user.
- **Empty states** teach: *"No events yet. Create your first one and tickets go on sale in minutes."* followed by a primary button.
- **Dates**: relative for near ("Tomorrow, 6:30 pm"), absolute for far ("Sat 14 Mar, 6:30 pm"). Always include the time zone for online events.
- **Money**: ledgers show full amounts with two decimals; marketing may drop trailing zeros. Always include the sign for deltas.
- **Counts**: "1 seat left", "8 seats left", never "1 seat(s)".
- **Confirmations for destructive actions** name the thing: "Delete 'Spring Gala'? 142 tickets are sold."

## Accessibility

- Text contrast of at least 4.5:1 (3:1 for large text and UI components). Do not use `{colors.subtle}` for required reading.
- Targets are at least 44×44px, with 8px between adjacent targets. The door screen uses 56px.
- Every interactive element has a visible focus ring (`{elevation.focus-ring}`) and a logical tab order. Modals trap focus and return it on close.
- Status is expressed with words and icons as well as colour (see Principles).
- Forms: labels are programmatically associated, errors are announced with `aria-live="polite"`, and required fields are marked in text, not by colour or asterisk alone.
- QR codes always come with a typed code (`ticket-code`) so check-in works if a camera fails or a phone cracks.
- Respect `prefers-reduced-motion` and `prefers-color-scheme`.
- Charts have text alternatives and a data table toggle.

## Responsive Behavior

| Name | Width | Changes |
|---|---|---|
| Mobile | < 640px | Single column, 16px margins, fluid cards, sticky 64px bottom tab bar, full-width primary buttons, tables become stacked cards, modals become bottom sheets |
| Tablet | 640-1023px | 24px margins, 6-column grid for card lists, collapsible navigation drawer, stat tiles 2-up |
| Desktop | 1024-1439px | Fixed 248px sidebar, 12-column grid with 24px gutters, 3-up event grid, 4-up stat tiles, ledger as a full table |
| Wide | ≥ 1440px | Content caps at 1280px and the page gains outer margin; type does not scale up |

Design the member app, ticket flow and check-in at 390px first, then scale up. Display type steps down 56 → 44 → 36px (`display-xl-mobile`) and 44 → 30px (`display-lg-mobile`); card grids drop columns rather than shrinking cards.

## Do's and Don'ts

### Do

- Use `{colors.primary}` for exactly one filled action per screen, and for links, focus and selection.
- Give each module its tint and keep it consistent across icons, cards and headers.
- Set every amount in a money token with tabular figures and a visible sign.
- Show a seat meter or stock count wherever availability limits a purchase.
- Pair each status colour with a word, and an icon where dense.
- Give the member pass, the ticket stub and the check-in screen a physical, tactile feel; they are the product's memorable moments.
- Provide an empty state, a loading skeleton and an error state for every list.
- Keep the QR code and its typed code together.
- Make the "who owes what" view one tap away for the treasurer.

### Don't

- Don't use red for ordinary expenses; reserve it for overdrawn balances, failed payments and invalid tickets.
- Don't use Skyline Blue as a card background, and don't introduce a second brand colour. Tints are identity; Sunset is a rare accent.
- Don't use the brand blue to mean "success", or green to mean "click".
- Don't round buttons into pills. Pills are for filters, status and search.
- Don't use `{colors.subtle}` for text people must read.
- Don't rely on colour alone to convey ticket validity, stock level or payment state.
- Don't hide the price. Show member and standard prices side by side, with the total before the pay button.
- Don't use horizontal scrolling for key tables on phones.
- Don't stack two primary buttons, or put a primary button inside a browsing card.
- Don't send the same message separately to each group. The composer targets audiences, and the feed keeps the record.
- Don't invert light mode to get dark mode, hard-code white text on the primary button, or use pure black or pure white in dark.
- Don't add decorative gradients, glassmorphism or confetti loops. Warmth comes from tints, type and tickets.

### Iteration tips

When something looks off, check in order: Is there more than one filled primary button? Is any amount missing tabular figures or a sign? Is a status communicated by colour alone? Is a module's tint mixed with another's? Is there any red on an ordinary expense? Fix those before adding any decoration.
